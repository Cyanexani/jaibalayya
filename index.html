The argument

Why Metro?

01

At a glance

A tile is a window, not a shortcut. Weather, unread counts, the next thing in your calendar and the song that is playing all live on the surface — so most of the time, you never open anything at all.

02

People first

Contacts are not a database. Metro OS puts the people you talk to at the centre of the shell: pin them to Start, see their messages and calls together, reach them in one tap from anywhere in the system.

03

Motion

Every transition tells you where you came from and where you are going. Content flies in from the direction of travel, headers lead, lists follow. Fast, direct, purposeful — animation as navigation, never as decoration.

04

Open

No account. No telemetry. No store lock-in. Metro OS is built in public by people who missed this interface and decided to rebuild it properly — every tile, every transition, every line of it readable on GitHub.

Open development

Built
in the open.

Every decision happens in issues and pull requests. The shell, the tile engine, the apps and this website are all public, all reviewable, all forkable.
