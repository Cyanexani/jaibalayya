Release history

  • NewA single tile scheduler for the whole device. Apps declare an update budget; the engine batches every wake into shared windows.
  • NewTile flip faces — a second face per tile, with staggered timing so the Start screen never pulses in unison.
  • NewWide-tile resize gesture with snapping, and drag-to-reorder that survives rotation.
  • NewPhotos and Music tiles render artwork directly, without waking the owning app.
  • ChangedAccent tokens are exposed to third-party apps through a content provider rather than a broadcast.
  • FixedBack stack could skip a frame when the app list was entered from search.
  • FixedNotification listener no longer loses its binding after aggressive OEM power management kills the shell.
