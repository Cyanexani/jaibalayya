Metro's transitions are not decoration: each one tells you where you came from. Below is the real thing — the same easing curves and stagger the shell uses, running live. Nothing here is a video.

01

Turnstile

Tiles pivot in from the left edge, staggered by 40 ms. Used when a list first appears.

02

Continuum

Content leaves in the direction of travel while the new page arrives behind it. Used between screens.

03

Tile flip

A tile turns on the X axis to show its second face. The engine staggers flips so the wall never pulses in unison.
