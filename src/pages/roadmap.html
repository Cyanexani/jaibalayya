Roadmap tracks

TRACK 01

Foundation

Complete

The shell itself: launcher surface, Start screen, back stack, gestures and the typography system.

  • Start screen and tile grid
  • Drag, resize, pin, unpin
  • App list with alphabet jump
  • Back stack that behaves

TRACK 02

System

Complete

Everything around the apps: notifications, quick settings, volume, multitasking and the lock screen.

  • Notification centre
  • Quick settings panel
  • Task switcher
  • Lock screen with glance data

TRACK 03

Apps

In progress · 8 of 13

The core suite. Eight applications ship in the preview; three are on branches and two are unclaimed.

  • Phone, Messaging, People
  • Music, Photos, Settings
  • Files, Browser, Camera
  • Maps, Store

TRACK 04

Experience

In progress

The part that makes it Metro rather than a skin: transitions, easing, tile animation and haptics.

  • Turnstile and continuum transitions
  • Tile flip scheduler
  • Per-app transition hand-off
  • Haptic vocabulary

TRACK 05

Devices

In progress

Hardware reality: display cutouts, gesture areas, refresh rates and the long tail of OEM behaviour.

  • Android 11+ baseline
  • Cutout and gesture insets
  • High refresh-rate tile updates
  • Tablet and foldable layouts

TRACK 06

Community

Ongoing

Open development: documentation, translations, design resources and a review process people can trust.

  • Public issue tracker and RFCs
  • Contributor guide
  • Translation pipeline
  • Design kit and icon source
