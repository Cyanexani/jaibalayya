## overview
Live Tile Studio lets you build a tile in HTML and CSS, preview it at every size, and pin it to start. Tiles run in a sandbox with no access to Metro OS or your data.

- Five templates to start from: big clock, countdown, day progress, note to self, gradient.
- Your tile gets data-size and --accent to adapt.
- “Copy as JSON” prepares it for the Store.
