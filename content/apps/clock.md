## overview
Clock has three pivots: **alarms**, **timer** and **stopwatch**. They run in the background of Metro OS, so a timer keeps counting and an alarm rings whatever app you have open.

- Alarms repeat on the days you choose, or ring once.
- The timer has presets (1 to 30 minutes) or any number of minutes.
- The stopwatch keeps laps with split times.
- The Clock tile shows the time and your next alarm.
- Hold the tile for the next alarm, a 5-minute timer and the stopwatch.

## what's new
### 0.2: live timer
- A running timer or stopwatch shows live on the Clock tile, or as a chip in the status bar when Clock isn’t pinned.

### 0.1: first version
- Alarms with repeat days and names; snooze for 9 minutes.
- Timer with a progress ring; starts from the Bloom in one tap.
- Stopwatch with laps.
- Alarms ring even when system sounds are off.

## improved
- A timer starts straight from start: hold the tile, tap *5-minute timer*.
- Missed alarms are reported honestly instead of ringing late.

## removed
Nothing removed.

## coming next
- World clocks.
- Choose the alarm sound.

## known issues
- Alarms and timers only ring while Metro OS is open in a tab. Browsers can’t wake a closed page, so an alarm that passes while it’s closed shows up as *missed*.
