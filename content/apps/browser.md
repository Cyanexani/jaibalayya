## overview
Browser reads Wikipedia inside Metro OS, with bookmarks and history. Other websites open in a normal browser tab, since most sites refuse to be shown inside another page.

- Type a web address to open it in a new tab.
- Metro OS links (#/…) open in place.
- Wikipedia text is shown with its CC BY-SA credit and a link to the full article.
