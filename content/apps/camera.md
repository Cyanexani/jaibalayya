## overview
Camera uses your webcam or phone camera. Photos and videos are saved to Photos in this browser. The camera switches off as soon as you leave the app.

- Photo and video modes.
- Switch between front and back cameras.
- The last photo opens in Photos from the thumbnail.
- Hold the tile to jump straight into photo, video or the front camera.
