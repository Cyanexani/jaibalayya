## overview
Settings is a pivot with two sides: **system** (start + theme, lock screen, you, sounds, about) and **applications** (every app with a link to its notes).

- 20 accent colours, dark and light themes, four fonts.
- Tiles in accent colour, each app's own colour, or see-through over a wallpaper.
- Hold the Settings tile to jump straight to accent colour, wallpaper or lock screen.

## what's new
### 0.1: first version
Settings is part of the first Metro OS release.

## improved
- Every settings page has its own link, e.g. `#/app/settings/lock`.
- Setup is optional and can be re-run from here as *make it yours*.

## removed
- The forced first-run setup. Visitors get a ready-made start screen instead.

## coming next
- Per-app settings under *applications*.
- Import and export your layout as a file.

## known issues
- A very large wallpaper photo may not fit in browser storage; Metro OS shrinks it first, but some browsers still refuse.
