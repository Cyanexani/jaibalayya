## overview
Maps shows OpenStreetMap, drawn with Leaflet. Search as you type, find yourself, save places, and hand off to OpenStreetMap for directions. In the dark theme the map dims to match.

- Search as you type (Photon, built on OpenStreetMap data).
- Save places; they stay in this browser.
- Directions open on openstreetmap.org.
- Every place has a link: #/app/maps/place/lat,lon.
