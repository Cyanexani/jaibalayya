## overview
The Metro OS app is the website, folded into a panorama. Swipe sideways through about, what's new, roadmap, apps, gestures, principles, get it, community, FAQ and credits.

- Its wide tile flips to show the latest release.
- Hold it for shortcuts to what's new, the roadmap, downloads and gestures.

## what's new
### 0.1: first version
Metro OS is part of the first Metro OS release.

## improved
- The old Metro OS website was eleven separate pages. Now it's one app you can swipe through, with a link to every section.

## removed
- The marketing hero and scroll effects from the old site. The live phone does that job now.

## coming next
- A "since your last visit" view that lists every change across all apps.
- Roadmap voting through GitHub reactions.
