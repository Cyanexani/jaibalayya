## overview
Notes keeps short notes in this browser. The first line is the title. Pin a note and it shows in the Notes Bloom and on the Notes tile.

- Saves as you type; an empty note is thrown away.
- Pin one note to keep it one hold away on start.
- Search finds text inside your notes.
